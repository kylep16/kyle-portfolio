## Earlier brand chapters (optional)

Add your first sketch, first sample, first shoot, an early release, or packaging/fulfillment photo. For each photo, include a year and one sentence explaining what it represents. Confirm the founding story and any milestones you want featured. The existing page uses November 2021 from your portfolio.

## PXI case study additions

To complete the new disc walkthrough, provide the resting frame, an interaction state, the resulting state, and a prototype recording if motion matters. Include:

- What the disc represents and what someone can do with it.
- The problem it solves and your design decision.
- Figma frame names or links, plus image exports if Figma access is unavailable.
- Status: exploration, prototype, implemented, or shipped.
- Which images are approved for the public portfolio.

For the redesigned camera, provide two filter-specific pill treatments and the post-capture state you want featured. Identify which design is current and whether it shipped. The page currently labels the older camera images as the shipped baseline and reserves separate slots for the redesign.
